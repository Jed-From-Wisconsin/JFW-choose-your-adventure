# 💪 Ground Team Hired

You hire some guys to take over the bank and secure the customers. You all pull up to the bank, and storm in. The customers are secure. , but you can tell that the tellers pressed their emergency buttons, so you have limited time to act. You command the bank manager to open the vault. With help only minutes away, he can tell that you and your men won't take any attempt to stall. He quickly opens the vault for you- as you walk inside, you realize it's a lot of money to take, and you may not be able to get all of it before the police come.

You could call over one of the guys to help bag the money, but that's one less person keeping the situation on the ground in check- on the other hand, you've come too far to literally just leave money on the table.
