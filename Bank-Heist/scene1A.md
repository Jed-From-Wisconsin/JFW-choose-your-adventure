# ☀️ Day Raid

You decide to break into the vault during the day- this means breaking in while employees are doing their jobs, and customers using the bank's services. A risky endeavor, but it also means you could use an employee to open the bank vault- no fancy explosives needed (or thermal drills that need to be repaired every 15 seconds, for that matter). 

The stakes are high- perhaps it's worth recruiting some people you know to assist you. If you're willing to give up a portion of your would-be spoils, you could hire some people to negate the risk. 

You could hire a guy to hack into the bank's security system, disabling the alarms, cameras, and emergency buttons, but you'll have to move fast, since you won't have anyone with you to keep the employees and customers in check.
