# The Hacker

You hire your small team, they are all well trusted friends. You guys only know one way into the bank: the vents. You dont have any idea of the layout of the vents, but your confident you can get through them, and once in, you know your boys will be able to deal with the guards if necessary.

You guys get to the vents, crawling one by one. Your boy at the front noticed a trigger on the vents, you dont know what its for, but you guys make sure to go a different way, after reaching the end of the vents, you guys drop down one by one. You make your way past the security guards, with your boys knocking them out along the way. You get to the vault door and it opens electronically with a key card. One of your guys breaks the key card box revealing two wire, red and blue.
