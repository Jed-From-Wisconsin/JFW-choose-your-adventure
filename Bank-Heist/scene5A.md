# The Hacker

You find a good hacker, you guys hack into the banks data, find the ins and outs of the layout, and feel ready to go in. 

With the layout you got from the hacker, you find you can go in through the vents like you originally intended, but he can also open up the staff only door in the back and disable all security cameras, the problem is, there are 2 security guards regularly patrolling these hallways.

You can also go through the vents, but there is a trigger to detect if somone sneaks through them, and knockout gas is sent through the vents. You dont know the correct path through the vetns to avoid this trigger, so it might be risky.
