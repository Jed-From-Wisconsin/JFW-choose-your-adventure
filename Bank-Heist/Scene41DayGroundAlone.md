# 🤐 Bag the money on your own

You decide to bag the money on your own- even if you might get less money, it's better than someone trying to be a hero, and making a mess of things. You manage to get all the money in the bag, but you've cut it very close- it took a significant amount of time to get everything, and by the time you leave, the cops were rapidly closing in. The rush to leave was frantic, and though you managed to narrowly escape, you realize that one of your men was left behind in the scuffle.

Inevitably, he rats you out, and it isn't long before the police track you down and arrest you and your buddies. 

So close!
