# You work alone

You decide to pull this thing off by yourself- if you could manage it, you'd be leaving that bank with a pilot's salary in your pockets.

You walk into the bank immediately with your weapon withdrawn, screaming for everyone to get on the floor, and demanding the manager. Everyone's on the floor, but you can't keep your eyes on everyone at once- inevitably, several customers are able to escape as you confront the bank's manager, and this guy's a total sleazeball- blabbing about time locks, a code that changes daily, that he as a branch manager has no access to the code-
By the time you catch onto what he's doing, you realize that someone's probably hit the emergency button by now, and the cops are probably coming in force. You take one look at the vault door, which seemingly taunts you at this point, and slip out.

Luckily, you're able to escape in your car unseen, which blends into traffic as you drive away, cops speeding past you. Only time will tell what'll happen, now- you'll be spending the next few years hoping to God this thing doesn't get traced back to you. Risking all that jail time, and nothing to even show for it.

The next time you try and rob a bank, consider that teamwork does indeed make the dream work.
