# Night Raid

You decide to raid the bank at night, no commotion and no risk with any civilians, seems to be safer. You now have to decide on a team, or should you go alone? The choices are a lot of pressure, the wrong choice and you could end up in jail for a long time. 

You think about it, you could hire a small team, you have a few friends who are ex military that could help you sneak in and hopefully not get caught through the back door, but you will trip the alarms either way. You could hire a hacker to disable the security system and alarms, and go by yourself, but then he cant enter with you, and you will have to go alone. 

You get the thought that maybe it might be less risky to go by yourself, and then you get more money as well in the end, no splitting the pot.

You think for a while, then make your decision:

2. [Hire a small team to help sneak in and take down any gaurds if necessary](./scene5B.md)
3. [Rob the bank alone](./scene5C.md)
