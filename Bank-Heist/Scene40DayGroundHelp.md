# Get help bagging the money

There's too much money to bag before the cops get here- you call one of your men over- the others could surely handle the crowd. With the both of you working to get the money in the bag, the vault gets cleared much faster. You manage to slip out in the nick of time, and manage to just barely avoid the cops. You drive over to a shady bar, and divide the money. 

More people involved means more people to divy the money to- you come away with $25,000. Not bad! You'll have to lay low for a bit before you can use any, but for the time being, you can be proud of your ill-gotten gains. Good job!
