# Full Team

You decide to hire both a hacker and ground team. As you and your collegeaus walked in and made themselves known, several bank employees attempted to sound the alarm, but your hacker had skillfully disabled all the security systems rendering them moot. 

The bank was locked down as everyone was held in the back room and kept under careful watch- you forced the manager to open the vault door, and you loaded up the bags with money. All of you left as quickly as you came with no trace, and by the time police could be called, you were long gone. Detectives were left scratching their heads, as there was absolutely nothing tracing the crime back to you.

After splitting the money several different ways, your share was only about 10,000 dollars... for the time and effort it took to plan this heist, and the risk you took pulling it off, it really wasn't worth it- maybe you could put a down-payment on a small house, or stick it into a Roth IRA, but you were probably better off just getting a second job at McDonald's or something.

Moral of the story? Crime truly doesn't pay.
